# Sandworth Homes — User Manual for Beginners

*Welcome to Sandworth Homes. This guide explains, in plain language, how the app works for people who are brand new to it. No technical knowledge is needed.*

---

## 1. What is this app?

Sandworth Homes is an online property platform where you can:

- **Browse homes, apartments and shops** — rent, buy, or lease commercial space.
- **Save** properties you like so you can find them again.
- **Book a viewing (tour)** of a property.
- **Apply** to rent an apartment and **pay your rent/move-in fees online**.
- **Make an offer** to buy a home that's for sale.
- **Message our team** (the leasing desk) — no phone tag, everything in one place.
- **See your whole history** with us — every application, payment, tour and message — every time you come back.

The app runs in a web browser. Nothing to install.

---

## 2. Opening the app

In your browser, go to:

```
http://localhost:8080/sandworth-zillow/index.php
```

This opens the **Home** page — the front door of the app.

---

## 3. Your account (registration & login)

You need an account to do anything useful (save, apply, pay, message).

### Create an account
1. Click **Get started** (top-right).
2. Fill in:
   - **Name**
   - **Email** (this will be your login)
   - **Phone number**
   - **Password** (choose something you'll remember)
3. Click **Create account**. You're now signed in automatically.

### Sign in later
- Click **Sign in** (top-right).
- Enter your **email** and **password**, then click **Sign in**.

### Sign out
- Click **Sign out** (top-right). Always sign out on shared computers.

> Your account remembers your name, saved properties, applications, payments and conversations — so when you come back, everything is still there.

## 5. Finding a home

- Click **Rent** to see apartments, **Buy** to see homes for sale, or **Malls & Shops** for commercial space.
- Use the search bar to type a **location** or a keyword.
- You can filter by **number of bedrooms** (e.g. "2+ beds") and other options.
- Click any property to open its **detail page** — it shows photos, price, location, bedrooms, bathrooms, and a list of features.

**Prices are shown clearly:**
- Rentals show a **monthly** price and an **annual** (per-year) price.
- Homes for sale show a **total asking price**.
- Each rental page also shows the **total due at move-in** (first rent + service charge + security deposit), so there are no surprises.

---

## 6. Saving a property (your shortlist)

On any property page, click **☆ Save this property**.

- The button becomes **★ Saved**.
- Your saved homes appear under **Saved** in the menu.
- Click the button again to remove it.

This is just a "bookmark" — it doesn't reserve anything or cost anything.

---

## 7. Booking a viewing (a tour)

Before renting or buying, you can visit the property in person.

1. On the property page, click **🔑 Book a site tour**.
2. Pick a **date** and a **time** that suit you.
3. Add any notes (optional), then click **Request tour**.

Your request shows on the **Tours** page with one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Requested** | We've received it, awaiting confirmation |
| **Scheduled / Confirmed** | We've agreed a date & time (shows the host) |
| **Completed** | You attended the viewing |
| **No-show** | Booked but you didn't attend |
| **Cancelled** | The tour was called off |

> Tip: If you can't make a confirmed tour, message us in advance — repeated no-shows aren't a good look. 😊

---

## 8. Applying to rent an apartment

When you've found the apartment you want:

1. On the property page, click **Apply for this rental**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Annual income** (rough is fine — it's used to check you can afford it)
   - **Employer** (your job / workplace)
   - **Move-in date** (when you want to move)
   - **Number of occupants**
   - Any **notes**
3. Click submit.

Your application appears on the **Dashboard** under **Your rental journey** with a status:

| Status | Meaning | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Waiting for review | Wait — we check your details |
| **Approved** | You're approved! | Pay your move-in fees online |
| **Active** | You've paid & moved in | Open your Tenancy app |

> **Good news:** if your income comfortably covers the rent (about 1.5× the annual rent), your application is **approved instantly** — no waiting, no phone calls. You can pay right away.

---

## 9. Paying online (move-in & rent)

Once your application says **Approved**, you can pay:

1. From the **Dashboard**, find your application and click **Pay move-in charges**.
2. You'll see a clear summary of what's due (first rent + service charge + security deposit).
3. Enter your **card details** (this is a demo, so any test card, e.g. `4242 4242 4242 4242`, works).
4. Click **Pay & activate tenancy**.

As soon as payment succeeds:
- Your application becomes **Active**.
- Your **Tenancy** account opens with a **ledger** (a record of everything you've paid).
- You can make further payments (e.g. next month's rent) from the **Tenancy** page.

**You CANNOT pay before approval.** If you haven't been approved yet and try to pay, you'll see:
> "This application is not ready for payment yet."

---

## 10. Your tenancy account (if you're a tenant)

Open **Tenancy** from the menu. Here you can:

- See the property you're renting, your move-in date and monthly rent.
- See your **ledger** — every payment you've made.
- **Post a new payment** (e.g. rent for the next month).

This is your running record with us.

---

## 11. Buying a home (making an offer)

Homes **for sale** don't have a "rent" button — instead:

1. On the home's page, click **Make an offer**.
2. Enter your **offer amount** (what you'd pay), a desired **timeline**, and any **terms** (e.g. "subject to mortgage approval").
3. Submit.

Your offers appear under **Offers**. The sales desk responds:

| Response | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Accepted** | Great — we've agreed; next steps toward contract |
| **Countered** | We came back with a different price/terms |
| **Declined** | We passed this time |

We recommend starting with the **Plan** page's **Mortgage & budget calculator** to check what you can afford before offering.

---

## 12. Messaging our team

On any property, click **💬 Message leasing desk**, or open **Messages** from the menu.

- Type your question and hit send.
- Our reply appears in the same conversation thread.
- A small number **in brackets next to Messages** (e.g. `Messages (2)`) means you have unread replies.

This keeps every conversation with the company in one place, attached to your account.

---

## 13. Your history ("what I've gotten from them")

Open **History** from the menu. This page is your permanent record with Sandworth Homes:

- A **summary** at the top: homes applied for, tenancies, **total paid**, properties saved, offers made, tours booked.
- A **full timeline** of everything you've done: account opened, tours booked/attended/missed, applications, approvals, tenancy activation, every payment, saved properties, offers, and messages.

Coming back later, this page reminds you (and our team) of your full history with us.

---

## 14. Quick troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Can't remember your password | (Demo) create a fresh account or contact the administrator. |
| I applied but there's no "Pay" button | Your application is still `Submitted`. Wait for approval (or bump your income so it qualifies). |
| I want to cancel a saved home | Open **Saved**, remove it — it's just a bookmark. |
| I missed my tour | It'll be marked **No-show**; message us to rebook. |
| I can't see Dashboard / Saved | Make sure you're **signed in**. |
